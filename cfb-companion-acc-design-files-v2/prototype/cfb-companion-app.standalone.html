Week 3 September 15, 2027

Clemson Crashes the Party as Conference Play Arrives

The Tigers finally return from an early-season layoff, Wake Forest keeps rolling into the national top 10, and the ACC opens its first real week of league games.

Clemson has been waiting patiently. After opening with a statement — a 62–7 burial of Arizona in Week Zero — the Tigers sat out two straight Saturdays while the rest of the league traded blows. That changes this weekend: Dabo's crew travels to the Dome for an 8 PM conference opener under the lights, carrying the ACC's stingiest defense (7 points allowed all season) into its first true test.

Meanwhile, Wake Forest has quietly become the league's standard-bearer. The Demon Deacons are 3-0, ranked ninth nationally, and just handled Utah State 21–10 on a short Thursday turnaround. Duke's offense deserves its own headline: 130 points through three weeks, including back-to-back explosions of 58 and 44, has the Blue Devils sitting at 3-0 with a marquee home date against NC State on deck.

This week marks the ACC's first real slate of conference games — six of them, spread from Durham to Berkeley. Louisville puts its 3-0 mark on the line at Stanford, Miami and Georgia Tech meet in Atlanta with both benches hungry, and California looks to stop a two-game skid against Boston College in the nightcap.

By Sunday night, we'll know whether Clemson's layoff was rust or rest, whether Duke is a legit Atlantic favorite, and whether anyone in the Coastal wants to act like they belong. Grab your snacks.
