Week 3 2027-09-15

Clemson Crashes the Party as Conference Play Arrives

The Tigers finally debut with a 62–7 haymaker, Wake Forest keeps rolling into the national top 10, and the ACC opens its first real week of league games.

Clemson has entered the chat. After sitting out the first two weekends, the Tigers made their 2027 debut count, burying Arizona 62–7 for the most lopsided result the ACC has posted all year. They weren't the only ones feasting: Duke hung 58 on Bowling Green, Louisville handled Nevada 29–17, and Wake Forest stayed perfect with a 21–10 win over Utah State.
